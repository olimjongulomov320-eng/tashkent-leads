Category: Barbershop (men's grooming + nail services, mid/premium)
Location: Two locations — Tashkent City and Broadway

## CURRENT DIGITAL PRESENCE
Website: none found
Instagram: @barbershop_unclechill, 21,000 followers
Other: phones +998 97 776-66-92 / +998 77 010-66-66; booking via DM only

## PROBLEMS
- No website — 21K followers with no owned booking infrastructure
- Two prime, high-traffic locations (Tashkent City and Broadway) have no single page listing both, hours, or contact
- Booking is entirely DM-based, a real bottleneck at this audience size
- No structured, browsable price list — services are scattered across Instagram posts

## OPPORTUNITY
Two locations in genuinely prime Tashkent real estate (Tashkent City, Broadway) plus a real 21K-follower audience is a strong commercial base with none of the infrastructure to match. A site with a location switcher and direct booking turns an Instagram-only operation into one with a real acquisition funnel independent of DM availability.

## DEMO CONCEPT
Bold, masculine, high-contrast — charcoal base with brass and ember accents, heavy condensed display type. The two-location structure is built into the hero itself (a location switcher, not an afterthought), and the price menu uses an alternating slide-in scroll reveal rather than a static table. The real 21K Instagram following is used as a headline stat moment.

## SALES ANGLE
Open with the two locations: "Tashkent City and Broadway are two of the best footfall locations in the city, and neither has a real online presence." Show the location-switcher hero directly addressing that. The DM-booking bottleneck is the second point — 21K followers deserves a booking flow that doesn't depend on someone answering Instagram messages.

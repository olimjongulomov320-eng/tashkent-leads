# EstiMED Clinic

## BUSINESS
Name: EstiMED Clinic
Category: Multi-specialty private clinic (dentistry, plastic surgery, urology, gynecology)
Location: Chilanzar district, ул. Мукими 11А, 1-квартал, Tashkent

## CURRENT DIGITAL PRESENCE
Website: estimed.uz (exists, dated)
Instagram: @estimed_clinic
Other: contact via phone / Telegram / WhatsApp only

## PROBLEMS
- Dated layout on the existing site — doesn't match the trust bar needed for high-ticket procedures
- No online booking of any kind — phone or messaging only
- Hours end at 17:00 Monday–Saturday, meaning every after-hours inquiry is lost outright
- Aesthetic (plastic surgery) and medical (urology/gynecology/dentistry) services are mixed together with no distinction in tone or presentation, despite having very different buyer psychology
- No visible doctor credentials, price transparency, or trust-building content online

## OPPORTUNITY
This clinic sells the highest-ticket services found anywhere in this research pass — yet closes its booking channel at 17:00 and relies on phone calls. A modern site with always-on booking directly closes a real, quantifiable revenue gap (every after-hours visitor is currently a lost lead), while a proper aesthetic/medical service split would let each buyer type see themselves reflected in the site's tone.

## DEMO CONCEPT
Clean, clinical, but genuinely calming rather than sterile — a single confident accent color on warm white, with the hero explicitly branching into separate aesthetic and medical service tracks. A 24/7 booking CTA directly addresses the real "closes at 17:00, phone only" problem, alongside a doctor-credentials trust section.

## SALES ANGLE
Lead with the math: "your highest-value services close their booking channel at 5pm." Show the demo's always-on booking widget as the direct fix. The aesthetic/medical split is the second selling point — a plastic surgery patient and a general dental patient shouldn't land on the same undifferentiated service grid.

# AnnTerra Beauty

## BUSINESS
Name: AnnTerra Beauty
Category: Premium founder-brand beauty salon (bridal + cosmetology focus)
Location: Nukusskaya 31/1, ЖК NRG Oybek, Mirabad district, Tashkent

## CURRENT DIGITAL PRESENCE
Website: none owned — only a generic booking-link-in-bio tool
Instagram: @annterra.beauty, ~9,300 followers
Other: phone not publicly found

## PROBLEMS
- Explicitly premium positioning with no premium web presence to match — a real brand/asset mismatch
- No owned site, only a third-party link-in-bio tool that looks the same as any budget salon's
- Bridal services (the highest-value offering) have no dedicated presentation — just posts mixed into the general feed
- No stylist-portfolio presentation despite Instagram highlights clearly showing individual stylist work

## OPPORTUNITY
A salon that explicitly markets itself as premium is currently indistinguishable, at the infrastructure level, from any budget competitor using the same generic booking tool. The gap between the brand's stated positioning and its actual digital presence is the entire pitch — closing it directly reinforces the price point they're already charging.

## DEMO CONCEPT
Editorial, restrained, quietly luxurious — cream and blush with a single refined rose/champagne accent, a thin elegant serif, large negative space. Bridal gets a dedicated three-act scroll sequence (preparation → ceremony → portrait) rather than a line item in a service list, and a stylist-portfolio rail gives individual talent the same visual weight their Instagram highlights already suggest exists.

## SALES ANGLE
Lead with the mismatch directly: "you call yourselves premium, but your booking page looks like every other salon's link-in-bio." The bridal-focused scroll sequence is the single strongest visual moment to show first — it's the highest-value service and currently has zero dedicated presentation anywhere.

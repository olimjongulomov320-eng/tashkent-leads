# Affresco Ristorante

## BUSINESS
Name: Affresco Ristorante
Category: Restaurant (premium Sicilian/Italian)
Location: Yakkasaray district, ул. Бабура 14, Tashkent

## CURRENT DIGITAL PRESENCE
Website: none found (only a Facebook page)
Instagram: not found
Other: listed on aggregators only — TripAdvisor, resto.uz, Facebook

## PROBLEMS
- No owned website at all — zero control over first impression or messaging
- Discovery depends entirely on third-party platforms (TripAdvisor, resto.uz)
- No online reservation flow — likely phone-only
- 15+ years of reputation not reflected anywhere it can be showcased on their own terms
- TripAdvisor rating (4.1) may understate the real dining room experience, since the aggregator narrative is the only one visible

## OPPORTUNITY
A restaurant ranked in the top 10% of Tashkent (#65 of 627 on TripAdvisor) with 119 reviews and 15+ years in business has already done the hardest part — earning trust. It has nothing online that reflects that. A real site would let them own their story, show the dining room and dishes on their own terms, and convert browsers into reservations without going through an aggregator.

## DEMO CONCEPT
Dark, warm, intimate — full-bleed inline-SVG compositions evoking candlelight, Sicilian ceramic tile, and a wine-dark palette. A serif display face with real Italian editorial character. The menu is presented as a scroll-driven typographic reveal through courses rather than a static PDF, and the real TripAdvisor rank is used as headline social proof.

## SALES ANGLE
Lead with the TripAdvisor ranking — "you're already top 65 out of 627 restaurants in Tashkent with zero website." Show them the demo next to their current Facebook-only presence. Emphasize that every competitor discovery point (TripAdvisor, resto.uz) sends traffic away from them, not to them — a real site with a reservation flow keeps that traffic and converts it directly.

# Express Beauty Team

## BUSINESS
Name: Express Beauty Team
Category: Beauty salon (multi-location, mid-market, family-oriented)
Location: Multiple locations across Tashkent (exact addresses not found)

## CURRENT DIGITAL PRESENCE
Website: none found
Instagram: @express_beauty_team, 46,000 followers
Other: booking currently happens via Instagram DM

## PROBLEMS
- No website at all despite the largest Instagram audience found in this entire research pass
- Booking runs entirely through DM conversations — a real conversion bottleneck at this follower count
- No structured price list or service menu anywhere outside individual Instagram posts
- No single canonical page listing all locations and hours
- Every booking currently costs a staff member's time in the DM queue

## OPPORTUNITY
46,000 followers is a serious, proven audience — the marketing problem is already solved. What's missing is the infrastructure to convert that audience into bookings without a human answering every DM. A site with a location picker and direct booking removes the single biggest point of friction in their entire funnel.

## DEMO CONCEPT
Bright, energetic, Instagram-native but elevated — coral and electric lime on a blush base, confident geometric display type. A horizontal scroll-snap service rail (not a static grid) and a hero location-picker wired directly into the booking CTA, plus a stylized "as seen on Instagram" grid that treats the existing 46K audience as the site's real social proof.

## SALES ANGLE
Open with the number: "46,000 people follow you and none of them can book without messaging you." Show how the demo's location-picker + booking CTA replaces the DM queue. This is the single strongest audience-to-infrastructure gap found in the whole research pass — lead with that framing.

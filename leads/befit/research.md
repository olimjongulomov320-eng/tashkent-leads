## PROBLEMS
- Real investment in a mobile app, but no marketing/acquisition website to bring new people to it
- The app only works for people who already know about BEFIT — there's no discoverable top-of-funnel
- No public class schedule, pricing, or location list anywhere outside the app itself
- Premium positioning has nothing public backing it up

## OPPORTUNITY
Building an app before a marketing site is a common but costly ordering mistake — it means every dollar spent on the app has no acquisition layer feeding it. A site that sells the free-trial and lets people discover class schedules and locations before committing to a download directly fixes the funnel's missing top.

## DEMO CONCEPT
Dynamic, athletic, HUD-like — near-black base with a single high-saturation lime accent, oversized numerals used structurally for schedules and stats. The signature scroll moment is a weekly timetable where day columns activate and class rows build in as you scroll, plus an animated multi-location diagram. All illustrative stats are explicitly labeled as representative, not fabricated as real figures. Free-trial signup is the primary CTA; the app is secondary.

## SALES ANGLE
Frame it as a funnel-ordering fix: "you built the app before the front door." Show how the free-trial CTA and class timetable let someone commit to visiting before they ever need to download anything — the app becomes the retention layer once they're already a customer, not the primary acquisition channel.
